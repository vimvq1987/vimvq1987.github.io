It all started 5 years ago.

But the beginning was much longer than that - maybe 35 years ago.

Even though I was born and raised in a country famous with coffee production, I was not really fond of it. My first coffee memory was with my dad. Growing poor, like many other of our neighbors in early 90 (and we were even amongst poorest in the neighborhood), coffee was a luxury, it was not something you took for granted. My dad was gifted by someone with a bag of coffee, and he stashed it away for most of the year. It's a treat on Tết holiday, a "Vietnamese phin" style. It smelled lovely, but also very bitter. But with a lot of sugar, it's drinkable. I was able to taste some, and while it's not something I enjoyed (more than candies), I was quite fond of it.

Fast forward 20 years, I am now a grown man working as a software companies. I am still not fond of coffee. The occasional coffee I drink is the instant coffee - coffee extract with a lot of milk power, and of course, sugar. It's sweet with some reminiscent of coffee, quick to prepare and easy to drink.
